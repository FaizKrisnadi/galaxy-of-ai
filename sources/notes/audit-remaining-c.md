# Reading audit: previously reviewed records, group C

Checked 2 November 2026 using original publication/author pages through web reader. This audit reads the named abstract or summary only; earlier catalogue claims of deeper reading are not independently verified here. Reported findings and authors' proposals are not independent confirmation.

- **cmu-state-local-ai-guide** — [CMU guide](https://www.cmu.edu/sites/default/files/cmu-block-center-site-files/documents/Safe%20and%20Equitable%20AI%20for%20State%20and%20Local%20Governments.pdf): read executive summary, PDF p. 1. Authors discuss community input and examples of local-government AI and recommend a national support strategy. Policy recommendations and selected examples are not a measured evaluation of nationwide impact; other pages unread in this pass.
- **ada-measuring-up** — [Ada Lovelace Institute](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/policy-briefing/measuring-up/): read key insights and opening of findings. Briefing scrutinises UK public-sector AI productivity estimates and recommends more rigorous measurement; this is a critical framework, not a definitive estimate. Body and underlying studies not audited in this pass.
- **olmo-paper** — [arXiv abstract, v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00838): authors present OLMo and release training data and training/evaluation code alongside model weights to enable scientific study. Read abstract only; code, data licence and full results not checked in this pass.
- **olmo3-blog** — [Ai2 announcement](https://allenai.org/blog/olmo3): read introductory description and initial model-flow claims. Ai2 describes sharing data, checkpoints and code for Olmo 3; performance claims are the developer's, not independently tested here. Technical report unread in this pass.
- **sealion-paper** — [arXiv abstract, v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05747): authors introduce two models adapted for 11 regionally relevant languages and report multilingual benchmark performance. Abstract only; their benchmark and performance claims not independently evaluated.
- **sealion-seald-dataset** — [AI Singapore project page](https://aisingapore.org/aiproducts/southeast-asian-languages-in-one-network-data-seald/): read overview and data sections. AISG and Google Research describe a multilingual data collaboration for training and evaluation, including nine listed languages; datasets and release/licence status not independently verified in this pass.
- **deepmind-stealth-sa** — [arXiv abstract, v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01420): authors propose evaluations for stealth and situational awareness and report no concerning levels in tested models. Their safety-case interpretation is an author claim about those evaluations, not proof that all deployments are safe; body and methods unread here.
- **ucl-iipp-netzero-dri** — [UCL repository abstract](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10196220/): Mazzucato discusses UK Research and Innovation and mission-oriented procurement for net-zero digital research infrastructure. Abstract only; recommendations are proposals rather than demonstrated impacts.
- **nus-genai-cs-education** — [arXiv abstract, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07545): a workshop-based white paper proposes shifting CS education toward design and evaluation of AI-generated artifacts. The catalogued HTML points to v1; this pass read v2 abstract only, without comparing versions or evaluating curriculum outcomes.
- **harvard-bkc-governed-ai** — [Berkman Klein summary](https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2025-12/are-we-ready-be-governed-artificial-intelligence): read institution's description of Schneier and Sanders' commentary on AI across US government and their call for decentralising power. Underlying essay unread in this pass; this is an argument, not observed nationwide effect.
- **meta-llama3-herd** — [arXiv abstract, v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783): developers present Llama 3 models and report evaluations including multilingual and tool-use capabilities. Model comparisons are authors' claims; full technical paper and benchmark methods not checked here.
- **microsoft-sparks-gpt4** — [arXiv abstract, v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12712): authors describe experiments on an early GPT-4 and argue its generality could be seen as an early, incomplete form of AGI. This is the authors' contested interpretation; full experiments unread in this pass.
- **stanford-canaries** — [Stanford publication summary](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/canaries-in-the-coal-mine-six-facts-about-the-recent-employment-effects-of-artificial-intelligence/): read summary. Authors report a relative employment gap for younger workers in AI-exposed US occupations using payroll data, with caveats including some pre-existing trends; they describe the evidence as descriptive, not causal. Full paper and data unread in this pass.
- **kaist-ussafebench** — [arXiv abstract, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15086): authors introduce a user-specific safety benchmark and report shortcomings among tested models. This is abstract-level reporting; the full benchmark design, measures and generalisability are not checked here.
- **eth-camel** — [arXiv abstract, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18813): authors propose a system layer separating trusted control flows from untrusted data to reduce prompt-injection risks; abstract reports an AgentDojo trade-off. Do not generalise its claimed guarantees beyond stated model and assumptions without reading the paper; body unread in this pass.
- **govai-voice-access** — [GovAI whitepaper overview](https://www.governance.ai/research-paper/voice-and-access-in-ai-global-ai-majority-participation-in-artificial-intelligence-development-and-governance): overview describes barriers to participation and proposals for access, voice and safety assurance. These are the authors' proposals; full whitepaper and underlying evidence unread in this pass.
- **govai-agents-infrastructure** — [arXiv abstract, v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10114): proposes external protocols and systems for agent attribution, interaction and remedy. Here infrastructure means shared technical systems and protocols, not a study of data-centre construction; full paper unread in this pass.
- **wadipalapa-indonesia-ai-policy** — [SAGE publisher abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/18681034231226393): interviews and documents in Jakarta, Central Java and East Java inform authors' analysis of central/local tensions in AI policy. Their case-study conclusions should not be represented as a nationwide measurement; body unread in this pass.
- **chula-ai-china-labor-thesis** — [Chulalongkorn thesis record and abstract](https://digital.car.chula.ac.th/chulaetd/74149/): thesis reports a provincial panel (2006–2022) and relationships between AI adoption, employment by skill and wages in China. Abstract-only here; identification and causal claims require full methods and data review.
- **scitech-asia-ai-literacy** — [journal abstract](https://ph02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/SciTechAsia/article/view/256785): authors survey 318 respondents in Thai industries and model associations among AI literacy, anxiety and acceptance. Abstract only; a survey association does not by itself establish causality or national representativeness.
