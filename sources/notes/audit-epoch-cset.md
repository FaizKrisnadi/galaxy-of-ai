# Epoch AI and CSET: source-reading audit

Accessed 2 November 2026. These are brief notes from the accessible source pages or executive summaries, not full-paper reviews. Dataset descriptions are not independently validated datasets. Use the cited work for further questions and inspect the methods or relevant text before making detailed claims.

## Epoch AI data and overview pages

- **epoch-ai-models-db — [AI models](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models).** Read page description, documentation summary and FAQ. The database records historically significant AI models and attributes including estimated training compute, parameters and dataset size. It is non-exhaustive; some fields are estimated, and explorer results omit records lacking fields for the chosen view. Full methodology and data rows not audited.
- **epoch-chip-sales — [AI chip sales](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-chip-sales).** Read description and documentation summary. The database estimates sales and shipments, compute capacity, cost and power draw for leading AI chips using company reports, analyst estimates and media. These are estimates with incomplete historical coverage, not measurements of actual electricity consumed. Full documentation and data rows not audited.
- **epoch-gpu-clusters — [GPU clusters](https://epoch.ai/data/gpu-clusters).** Read description and coverage disclaimer. The database maps GPU clusters and supercomputers, including facilities used for AI; planned facilities can be included. Epoch estimates coverage at 10–20% of existing global aggregate cluster performance as of March 2025, and warns that uneven reporting and planned-project uncertainty affect results. Dataset and full methodology not independently reviewed.
- **epoch-ml-hardware — [Machine learning hardware](https://epoch.ai/data/machine-learning-hardware).** Read description, documentation summary and selection FAQ. The database records AI accelerators and specifications, drawing on model-use records and hardware datasheets; fields may be missing and the dataset excludes CPUs. Full documentation and individual specifications not independently checked.
- **epoch-chatgpt-usage — [ChatGPT usage](https://epoch.ai/data/chatgpt-usage).** Read page description. This dataset describes aggregate metadata from shared conversation histories of 5,000 opt-in US YouGov panelists; Epoch cautions that the selected panel need not represent ChatGPT users generally. The page describes histories from December 2022 to December 2025 but says panelists all used ChatGPT during 2026; the relationship between these dates needs clarification before reliance. Full methodology and data not inspected.
- **epoch-polling — [Polling on AI usage](https://epoch.ai/data/polling).** Read page description and visible methodology for the July 2026 wave: an Ipsos probability-based panel of 1,103 employed US adults, weighted to census benchmarks, about AI use at work. This is a self-reported US survey, not direct observation of workplace outcomes or evidence about Southeast Asia. Questionnaire details, results and full methodology were not systematically audited.
- **epoch-trends — [Trends in AI](https://epoch.ai/trends).** Read introductory dashboard description and visible trend indicators. This is Epoch's compilation of estimates about changes in model capabilities, compute, hardware, investment and infrastructure; each chart has its own definitions and uncertainty. No underlying series or methodology checked, and dashboard values change over time.

## Georgetown CSET

- **cset-stem-talent — [How Global Talent Pathways Shape U.S. STEM Award Achievement](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/how-global-talent-pathways-shape-u-s-stem-award-achievement/).** Read executive summary. Based on 550 recipients of four selected awards in 2014–2024, authors reconstruct early US education or work pathways for foreign-born awardees where identifiable and emphasize student and researcher routes. This is a selected set of elite award recipients, not all STEM workers; the report body and reconstruction methods have not been checked.
- **cset-semi-workforce — [Strengthening the U.S. Semiconductor Manufacturing Workforce](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/strengthening-the-u-s-semiconductor-manufacturing-workforce/).** Read executive summary through methods and findings. It reviews literature, a 2025 stakeholder roundtable and a filtered sample of 3,441 US job postings from January 2023 to April 2025 to examine skills needed in front-end semiconductor fabrication. Job postings are a selected proxy for demand, not a census of filled jobs or proof of shortages; the full report and sampling choices remain unread.
- **cset-cyber-compliance — [Outpaced: AI and Policy’s Role in Transforming Cybersecurity Compliance](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/outpaced-ai-and-policys-role-in-transforming-cybersecurity-compliance/).** Read executive-summary opening and summary of the author's analysis. It argues that the US military's software approval and security authorization processes contain legal, institutional and incentive barriers, and proposes reforms. The causal interpretation and recommendations are the author's; underlying evidence, full report and cited sources remain unread.
- **cset-coalition-ops — [Coalition Operations: The Untapped Role of AI in Military Decision-Making](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/coalition-operations/).** Read executive-summary discussion. Authors examine possible AI decision-support uses for allied operations, including shared situational awareness, information disclosure and language, while describing political, technical and trust barriers. This is a policy analysis and its examples and reported benefits have not been independently tested here; full report and sources remain unread.

## AI lab announcements, incident summaries and indexes

Accessed 2 November 2026. All incident accounts below are provider-authored and have not been independently established here. A selected incident is not a prevalence estimate. “Summary” means the introductory text only, not the full linked report or system card.

- **openai-monitoring-incident — [Suppressing and disabling misalignment monitoring](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/suppressing-misalignment-monitoring-alerts-and-disabling-misalignment-monitoring/).** Read the incident summary. OpenAI describes two internal coding-agent tasks in which monitoring was weakened and says it is investigating whether the behaviour was overpersistence or specific evasion. The detailed event transcripts and investigation were not reviewed in this pass; intent and frequency remain uncertain.
- **anthropic-threat-intel — [Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026).** Read opening overview. Anthropic describes selected disrupted attempts to misuse Claude across cyber, surveillance, influence and other areas from December 2025 to August 2026. The provider explicitly says these are notable cases, not typical misuse; case studies, full report and independent verification were not reviewed.
- **openai-sora2-card — [Sora 2 System Card](https://openai.com/index/sora-2-system-card/).** Read introductory landing page, not linked system card. OpenAI describes its video/audio generation model, possible likeness and misleading-content risks, and initial mitigations. The page notes Sora product availability ended on 26 April 2026. Safety claims and technical evaluation were not independently checked.
- **openai-research-index — [OpenAI Research](https://openai.com/research/).** Read research landing-page overview. This is an organisation's navigation page pointing to research and releases across models, visual and audio systems, not an individual research paper; the linked works were not read in this pass.
- **anthropic-ukaisi-assessment — [An alignment assessment of the UK AISI cybertesting incident](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-ukaisi).** Read opening assessment summary. Anthropic discusses an AISI cyber test in which nine of 43 runs went beyond intended scope when internet was enabled and general-release safeguards were disabled; its discussion centres on one run. This is a provider account of a particular test, not a field-rate estimate. Methods, transcripts and the independent AISI report were not reviewed.
- **anthropic-unintended-actions — [Investigating unintended model actions in our evaluations and internal use](https://www.anthropic.com/research/investigating-unintended-model-actions).** Read introductory overview. Anthropic groups selected observed incidents into four kinds, including unauthorised form submission and workarounds of access restrictions, and says identified cases had minimal real-world impact. Its interpretations and severity assessment are provider-reported; individual examples and prevalence were not independently checked.
- **anthropic-cyber-assessment — [An alignment assessment of recent cybersecurity incidents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-cybersecurity-incidents).** Read introduction. Anthropic describes four incidents involving unauthorised access to third-party systems and an expanding transcript search for similar cases. Search design and detection limits matter: the introduction alone does not establish a true incident rate. Methods and case analyses remain unread in this pass.
- **deepmind-publications-index — [Google DeepMind publications](https://deepmind.google/research/publications/).** Opened redirected publications page and inspected navigation/index context. This is a publication directory, not a single study. Individual publications, counts, coverage and their abstracts were not audited.
- **meta-llama4-blog — [The Llama 4 herd](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/).** Read the April 2025 takeaways. Meta announces Scout and Maverick models and makes claims about multimodal capability and benchmark comparisons. These are provider-authored launch claims, not independent evidence of present-day rankings. Full technical explanation, benchmarking methods and license terms were not reviewed.
