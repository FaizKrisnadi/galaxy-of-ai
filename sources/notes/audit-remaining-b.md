# Source-reading audit: remaining records 21–40

Accessed 2 November 2026. These notes concern the accessible original landing page, introduction or abstract, not a fresh full-document review. Prior catalogue notes may reflect deeper reading; they remain separate. Statements are source-reported unless otherwise specified.

- **iea-data-centres** — [IEA tracker](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/buildings/data-centres-and-data-transmission-networks). The page offers a 2015–2025 data-centre electricity-consumption chart, drawing on the IEA's 2026 Energy and AI questions. Read: tracker description and source attribution. Chart values and methodology not audited here.
- **ibm-what-is-ai** — [IBM explainer](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/artificial-intelligence). The introduction explains AI and positions machine learning and deep learning as related concepts, with examples of generative models. Read: introduction and introductory machine-learning sections. This vendor explainer is not empirical evidence for every claim; remainder not systematically assessed.
- **uob-dbs-dayone-ndp-loan** — [DBS newsroom](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_UOB_arrange_record_IDR_6_7_trillion_SGD_530_million_loan_for_data_centre_development_project_by_DayOne_and_the_Indonesia_Investment_Authority). DBS reports that it and UOB provided a 6.7-trillion-rupiah facility for a Batam data-centre campus jointly developed by DayOne and INA; proceeds support three Nongsa Digital Park centres. Read: opening paragraphs of lender announcement. Lending terms and completion not independently established.
- **epoch-ai-data-centers-db** — [Epoch data centres](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-data-centers). Epoch describes an independently estimated database of major AI data centres using satellite imagery, permits and assumptions about facility construction. Read: page introduction. Underlying entries and estimates not audited in this pass.
- **cset-redteaming-reporting** — [CSET summary](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/systematizing-the-reporting-of-ai-red-teaming/). The authors address variation in AI red-teaming practices and reporting. Read: publication-page overview only, not full report in this pass.
- **cset-tracking-chips** — [CSET summary](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/tracking-ai-chips/). The report considers chip location verification as a possible export-control enforcement approach. Read: publication-page overview; this framing is the authors' policy perspective, not an independently established estimate of smuggling. Full report unread in this pass.
- **cset-ai-competition** — [CSET summary](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/artificial-intelligence-in-competition/). The authors argue that AI capabilities do not automatically yield competitive benefits and compare ways to connect development and use to intended outcomes. Read: publication overview only; report body not reassessed.
- **hai-ai-index-2026** — [Stanford HAI report landing page](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report). Read: report overview and introductory highlights only; underlying studies and full report not rechecked.
- **openai-long-horizon-safety** — [OpenAI summary](https://openai.com/index/safety-alignment-long-horizon-models/). Company claims and context not independently validated.
