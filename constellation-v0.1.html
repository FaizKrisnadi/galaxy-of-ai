Galaxy of AI — first constellation

A small, explorable map for one learner: AI at the centre, and its connections to data & compute, data centres, electricity, place, economics, jobs, governance and human oversight. Click any node to read a short, source-linked explanation.

How to read the lines: topic link means two subjects are related — not that one causes the other. fact (sourced) points to something documented. open question is something nobody has settled here.

What this is: a personal learning prototype (v0.1, 1 Nov 2026), not a research instrument. Single HTML file — no framework, no accounts, no paid APIs, no external assets; it loads nothing from the internet (source links open only if you click them).

Honest framing: topic links mean “these subjects are related”, never “this causes that”. Facts are summaries of the linked sources — check the originals before relying on them. Existing tools that inspired this (all mapped in PRE-STUDY.md): MIT AI Governance Map, OECD AI Policy Navigator, OpenAlex Topics, ArusGrid, Open Knowledge Maps. Nothing here claims to be novel research.
