# Galaxy of AI

Galaxy of AI is an early, personal map for exploring how artificial intelligence connects to infrastructure, work, society, economics, governance and the environment. Select a planet to explore its topics, and select a topic for an explanation and links to sources. The astronaut opens search and guided paths.

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No account or installation is required. On smaller screens, the topics appear in a list.

## About the research

This is a work in progress, not an exhaustive or independently peer-reviewed reference. Source records have different levels of review: some are read in full or in part, others are indexed or based on abstracts. Links between topics can represent an association, an argument or an open question; they do not by themselves establish causation. Follow the linked sources and check their dates, methods and limitations before relying on a claim.

The map is a snapshot. Its figures and links can become outdated, and the underlying research collection is not included in this preview. Downloaded source publications are not included.
