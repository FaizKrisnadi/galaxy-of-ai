# Galaxy of AI

A personal map for exploring how artificial intelligence connects with technology, people, institutions, politics, economics and the physical world. This first constellation is a sketch for exploration, not a comprehensive map or a claim of novelty.

## Starting constellation

- **AI foundations:** models, training, inference, data, evaluation and agents.
- **People and institutions:** work, human judgment, public agencies, universities and organisational capacity.
- **Governance and law:** oversight, procurement, accountability, regulation and standards.
- **Physical infrastructure:** computing hardware, data centres, electricity, water, land and telecommunications.
- **Economics and politics:** finance, ownership, industrial policy, international relations and development.
- **Environment:** energy systems, emissions, resources and environmental governance.

These areas overlap. A connection means subjects are related; it does not automatically mean one causes another.

## Three paths to explore

1. **From a question to physical infrastructure:** AI request → model inference → computing hardware → data centre → electricity and cooling. A general learning path; it does not identify where any particular request runs.
2. **From AI to institutional control:** AI system → public agency → supplier relationship → monitoring and human intervention. What a particular agency can do depends on its actual arrangements.
3. **From data centres to place:** data-centre development → water and energy systems → public planning → residents and businesses. Local effects must be established case by case.

- [ArusGrid methodology](https://arusgrid.com/methodology): scope and methods of an existing data-centre project tracker.
- [Katadata: A Digital Boom in a Thirsty City](https://katadata.co.id/data-center-batam/en): reporting on Batam, data centres and water. Reported water insecurity does not by itself establish data-centre causation.
- [METR: Red-Teaming OpenAI's Internal Agent Monitoring Systems](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-22-red-teaming-openai-agent-monitoring/): a dated assessment of monitoring infrastructure; its findings concern the historical system assessed, and subsequent fixes were not independently checked by METR at publication.
